About Torii Health

A Medical Home for SOFA Americans in Japan

Torii Health was created because too many SOFA Americans in Japan—especially Space-Available families—do not have reliable access to comprehensive, relationship-based primary care.

Book an Appointment Meet the Team

Why Torii Health was built

While working as a family physician at Camp Zama, Dr. Kenji Taylor saw how often Space-Available patients struggled to get timely, consistent care. His own family experienced the same challenges while navigating pregnancy and childbirth in Japan.

Over time, it became clear that this was not an individual problem. Many Americans in Japan were left without a true medical home—a team that knew them, followed their health over time, and could provide comprehensive care in English.

Torii Health was created to fill that gap.

Background: JCMA’s analysis of access to care for SOFA families

Torii Health + Iris Dental Medical

Torii Health is part of Iris Dental Medical.

Together, we provide medical and dental care from one local practice near Camp Zama, supported by the clinical relationships and services needed to care for patients across Japan.

Torii Health’s goal is to provide as much care as possible through one familiar team—and remain involved when testing or specialty care needs to happen elsewhere.

Our Mission

To create a medical home for SOFA Americans in Japan built around relationships, continuity, and the needs of the community.

  • Comprehensive Family Medicine

    Care for children, adults, and families across the lifespan.

  • Integrated Behavioral Health

    Behavioral healthcare embedded within primary care, with counseling, medication management, and ongoing follow-up working together.

  • Connected Care

    One team that stays involved, coordinates next steps, and supports you when care extends beyond our clinic.
