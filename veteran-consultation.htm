Primary Care for U.S. Veterans in Japan

Veterans living overseas need consistent healthcare—not just help with paperwork.

Torii Health provides ongoing primary care for U.S. Veterans living in Japan, with attention to chronic conditions, service-related health concerns, continuity of treatment, and care that stays connected over time.

How we support Veterans

  • Comprehensive primary care

    Routine and preventive care, chronic disease management, medications, behavioral health, and evaluation of new health concerns.

  • Continuity for complex histories

    We review relevant history and records so that symptoms, diagnoses, treatments, and functional concerns are understood in the context of your ongoing care.

  • VA-related care and documentation

    When clinically relevant, we can review records, help coordinate VA-related healthcare, and make sure appropriate clinical information is reflected in the medical record.

Important note on DBQs and nexus letters

We generally do not provide standalone nexus letters or independent DBQs. Our focus is an appropriate clinical relationship centered on diagnosis and treatment.

When VA-related medical opinions or supporting documentation are appropriate, they are based on available records, clinical findings, and the care relationship developed over time.

Questions about whether our care is the right fit? Email info@toriihealth.org or see our FAQ for Veterans.
