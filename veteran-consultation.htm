Veteran Consultations

Dr. Kenji has extensive experience reviewing VA disability cases, including service as a Primary Care Manager at Camp Zama, Japan, and work as an independent medical evaluator for QTC and VES throughout Asia. This experience provides insight into how medical evidence is reviewed, where claims commonly encounter difficulties, and how clinical findings are interpreted during the evaluation process.

Many Veterans face challenges not because their condition lacks medical validity, but because the medical record does not clearly document the relationship between symptoms, diagnoses, and service history. This issue can be even more pronounced for Veterans living in Japan, where access to VA resources and continuity of care may be limited.

Dr. Kenji’s focus is on careful clinical evaluation, clarifying the medical record, and identifying gaps in documentation. When appropriate, this may help ensure that a Veteran’s condition and functional limitations are accurately reflected in the medical record.

The Service

Each case is reviewed individually. We generally do not provide standalone nexus letters or independent DBQs. In our experience, many of these reports are produced without sufficient clinical context and often carry limited value when claims are reviewed.

Our approach is different. When appropriate, we may provide guidance on navigating the VA disability process or review documentation to help you understand what information may be helpful in your case.

In some situations, support documentation may arise through an established clinical relationship focused on diagnosis and treatment. When care is provided through our primary care services, any medical opinions or supporting documentation are based on the medical record and clinical findings developed over time.

If you would like more information, please email info@toriihealth.org with a brief description of your situation and what type of assistance you are seeking. We will respond with additional details about whether our services may be appropriate for your case.
