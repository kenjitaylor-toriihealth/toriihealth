Family Medicine in Japan That Feels Familiar

Torii Health, part of Iris Dental Medical, provides English-language family medicine near Camp Zama and by telehealth throughout Japan.

We provide as much of your care as possible within one connected team, and coordinate outside testing or specialty care only when it is needed.

The Torii Health approach

  • Comprehensive Family Medicine

    Preventive care, acute illness, chronic disease management, pediatrics, physicals, vaccinations, and ongoing care across the lifespan.

  • Women’s & Reproductive Health

    Preventive care, contraception, menopause care, sexual health, and common reproductive-health concerns.

  • Behavioral Health

    Evaluation and treatment for conditions such as anxiety, depression, ADHD, PTSD, and related concerns, with counseling and medication management integrated into primary care.

  • Testing & Specialty Coordination

    Laboratory testing, imaging, endoscopy, mammography, sleep testing, pulmonary testing, and specialist care coordinated when clinically needed.

  • Minor Procedures

    Selected in-office procedures such as joint injections, cryotherapy, skin biopsy, and other appropriate outpatient procedures.

  • Medications

    Prescription management, selected in-clinic medications, refill follow-up, and coordination with military or outside pharmacies when needed.

A closer look at our care

Common services our patients ask about. Don’t see what you need? Ask our team—we coordinate many other services when clinically appropriate.

Preventive & everyday care

  • Check-ups and physicals, including school and sports physicals
  • Health screenings following U.S. guidelines
  • Vaccinations
  • Pediatric care
  • Chronic condition management

Sexual & reproductive health

  • Family planning and contraception
  • Menopause and perimenopause care
  • Erectile dysfunction treatment
  • STI testing, prevention, and treatment, including PrEP and PEP

Hormones, weight & gender-affirming care

  • GLP-1 medications for weight management when clinically appropriate
  • Testosterone therapy, lab monitoring, and follow-up
  • Hormone therapy for gender-affirming care

Behavioral health

  • Anxiety, depression, and PTSD
  • ADHD evaluation and treatment for children and adults
  • Addiction treatment
  • Counseling with our behavioral health team

Stimulant medications cannot be prescribed in Japan. We can prescribe non-stimulant options and, when possible, coordinate stimulant prescriptions through U.S. military providers.

Skin care & minor procedures

  • Acne treatment
  • Joint injections
  • Cryotherapy
  • Skin (shave) biopsy

Testing & screening coordination

  • Colonoscopy and upper endoscopy (EGD) with sedation
  • Mammography
  • Low-dose CT lung cancer screening
  • Home sleep studies and pulmonary function testing
  • X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound when clinically indicated

Medications & pharmacy

  • On-site medications whenever possible
  • Mail delivery of medications outside the Zama/Atsugi area
  • Electronic prescriptions to U.S. military pharmacies or Express Scripts when possible
  • Help finding an alternative source when a medication is not available in Japan
